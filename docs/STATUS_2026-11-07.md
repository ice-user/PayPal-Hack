### Backend (`backend/precedent/`)
- **Order feed and detection.**
  - A seeded synthetic order and fulfilment generator plants a known cause.
  - The Situation Builder computes Wilson intervals and a binomial test, then
    carrier/SKU concentration, carriers elevated (lost parcels only), ship delay and
    delivered share.
  - It reproduces ground truth on all 21 scenarios.
- **API (FastAPI).** Bundles are computed live. It handles approve/reject, the simulated
  clock, cases, the audit log, live dispute reads, a verified webhook receiver, and demo reset.
- **Executor.** It runs a state machine keyed by idempotency key, so a triple click executes
  once. The gate is re-run at approval, a rate limit applies, and every step is written to
  the SQLite audit log.
- **PayPal adapter.**
  - It talks to the sandbox host only and refuses a live host.
  - It re-reads the dispute before each action and makes the real call when PayPal allows it.
  - Otherwise it records a labelled simulation with the reason.
- **Outcome tracker (simulated clock).** It replays the merchant for 14 days and records
  before/after. It also creates a `live_simulated` case pending review, which is never
  retrievable until reviewed.
